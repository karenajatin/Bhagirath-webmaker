Shipping & Delivery Policy

Orders placed on LiveShop Platform India are confirmed by phone or WhatsApp before they are packed.

Delivery: we deliver to addresses in and around our city. Most orders are delivered within 1–3 working days after confirmation. Delivery charges, if any, are shown at checkout.

Pickup: you can choose “Pickup from store” at checkout and collect your order from our shop once we tell you it is ready.

If an item becomes unavailable after you order, we will call you and offer a replacement or remove it from your order.

Questions? Contact us.
