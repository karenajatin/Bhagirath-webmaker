About LiveShop Platform India

LiveShop Platform India is a website where local shopkeepers open their own shop and sell online. Every shop has its own page with its products, contact details and social media.

You order directly from the shop on WhatsApp and pay the shop by UPI or cash.

Contact

To ask about an item or an order, contact the shop directly from its shop page.
